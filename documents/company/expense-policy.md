# Expense Policy

Effective January 2026. Applies to all staff.

## Travel

Mileage is reimbursed at 67 cents per mile for personal vehicle use on company business. Parking and
tolls are reimbursed at cost with receipts.

Air travel is booked through the office manager. Economy fares only for flights under six hours.

## Meals

Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day, with receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Client meals require a business purpose noted on the expense report, and receipts over 100 USD
require partner approval.

## Equipment

Purchases under 250 USD may be made directly and expensed. Between 250 and 2,000 USD requires
manager approval. Above 2,000 USD requires written approval from a partner before the purchase.

## Submission

Expense reports are due by the fifth of the following month. Late submissions are still reimbursed but
are flagged in the monthly review.
