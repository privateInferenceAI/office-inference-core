# Office Hours and Coverage

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm Eastern.

## Front desk coverage

Two people cover the front desk at all times. Lunch coverage runs from 12:00pm to 1:00pm and is
scheduled a week ahead.

## Remote work

Staff may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Client-facing meetings are
in-person unless the client agrees otherwise.

## After hours

Voicemail is checked each business morning. True emergencies reach the on-call partner through the
answering service.
