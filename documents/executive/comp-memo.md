# Partner Compensation Memo

CONFIDENTIAL. Access is limited to partners and the office administrator.

## Base compensation

Partner base draw is 425,000 USD annually, paid monthly. Adjustments are reviewed each January by the
compensation committee.

## Profit distribution

Distributions follow the formula in the partnership agreement, weighted by originations and
collections. The weighting is reviewed every three years.

## Deferred compensation

Deferred amounts vest over five years. A partner who leaves voluntarily forfeits unvested amounts.
